Philosophy & Dedication

Every recipe begins with pure cream dairy butter, single-origin espresso, and slow dedication in Kandy.

Founded moments from Kandy Lake and Queen's Hotel, Café KOI and our custom studio KOI Cakes bring together specialty coffee craft, cozy cycling aesthetics, and scratch-baked cakes. From our celebrated wild blueberry cheesecake to bespoke multi-tiered wedding showstoppers, every creation is baked fresh with genuine care.
